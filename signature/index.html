Signature generator

Fill in your details, copy the signature, and paste it into Gmail. It takes about two minutes and works on Gmail for the web, iPhone and Android.

[ 02 ]  Preview and copy

Fill in at least your name and email to copy. The preview is exactly what gets pasted into Gmail.

[ 03 ]  Install in Gmail
01Fill in your details and click Copy signature.
02Open Gmail on a computer. Click the gear icon, then See all settings.
03On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it KrathVeil.
04Click inside the signature box and paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V. The black card appears with the logo.
05Under Signature defaults, choose KrathVeil for new emails and for replies and forwards.
06Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save Changes.
07Send an email to yourself and open it on your computer and your phone.
[ 04 ]  On your phone

The Gmail app on iPhone and Android uses your web signature when the app's own mobile signature is empty. Open the Gmail app, go to Settings, choose your account, and make sure Mobile signature is off or blank. Nothing else to install.

[ 05 ]  Good to know

Write your designation the way it appears on your offer letter, for example Founder & CEO or Systems Engineer. To change anything later, come back to this page, update your details, copy again and replace the signature in Gmail. Do not edit the signature by hand inside Gmail.

[ 06 ]  Problems

If the copy button does nothing, use Chrome on a computer. If the logo does not appear in Gmail, click Display images below on the email, or copy the signature again from this page. Anything else: connect@krathveil.com.
