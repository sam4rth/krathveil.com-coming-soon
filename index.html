01·The Fabric

What KTAF is, in one line.

KTAF, the Krath Tactical Adaptive Fabric, turns any radio, drone, or sensor into one AI-coordinated, jam-resilient mesh.

KTAF does not replace radios. It makes whatever radios you already have behave as one intelligent network.
02·Capabilities

Three things the fabric does, above whatever you already field.

01

Unified situational awareness

ATAK-native by default. Every node, handheld, vehicle, drone, and sensor pushes position, status, and Cursor-on-Target into one shared operational picture. Voice becomes one channel, not the only channel.

02

Transport-agnostic interop

Runs on LoRa, MANET WiFi, HaLow, LTE, SATCOM, and SDR. Bridges into VHF, UHF, HF, and the BEL tactical radio family. Any radio, any band, any backhaul; one coordination layer above all of them.

03

Jam-resilient, sovereign-controlled

Frequency agility, low-probability-of-intercept LoRa physics, and a mesh that self-heals under contested RF. Indian source code, Indian-controlled servers, Indian supply chain.

03·Architecture

Four layers. Well-defined interfaces.

L4Application
ATAK / KV-TAK fork, drone GCS, sensor management, C2. Third-party mission software via a published API.
L3Coordination
The defensible core. Unified node identity and cryptographic auth, adaptive multi-path routing across every transport, mission-priority QoS. AES-256-GCM, X25519 key exchange.
L2RF Awareness
Sees the spectrum. Passive spectrum monitoring, per-link quality scoring, jamming detection with direction estimation, anomaly classification.
L1Transport
Any bearer. LoRa, OpenMANET, HaLow, LTE / 5G, SATCOM, SDR. Bridges into VHF, UHF, HF, and the BEL tactical radio family.

Defensibility lives in Layers 2 and 3: the coordination intelligence and the operational data feeding the awareness engine. We ship the whole stack, Indian.
